# ADR-009: Dev/test split + dual metric families

Status: Accepted

## Context
Must show retrieval-quality improvement without leaking or tuning on the test set.

## Decision
Seed-42 eval pool; **dev (200)** for all tuning; **test (400)** for reported numbers only.
Report three families: LLM-judged RAGAS (context precision/recall), non-LLM RAGAS, and
classical IR (Hit@5, P@5, R@5, MRR@10, nDCG@10).

## Consequences
- Non-LLM and IR metrics are rate-limit-proof and cheap; LLM-judged numbers satisfy the
  brief on a documented 50-query sample.
